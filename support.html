Sweep: Floor Coverage Map

Common questions

The head is not found

Hold the phone so the floor head stays in view about a metre ahead. Redo the one-time setup from cleaning distance (Settings → Replace saved head) and wait for the green "Selection looks good" hint before saving.

The green trail is in the wrong place

Give Sweep a few seconds looking at the floor after tapping Start so it can measure the floor height. Sweep works best on iPhones with LiDAR (Pro models).

Restoring Sweep Pro

Settings → Restore purchase. Sweep Pro is tied to your Apple ID and works on all your iPhones.

See what you actually cleaned

Sweep paints the path of your vacuum's floor head onto your floor in augmented reality while you clean, and adds up the area you covered. Everything runs on your iPhone.

How it works

  • Point your iPhone at the floor while you vacuum.
  • Sweep follows the floor head and paints every strip it covers in green.
  • Finish to see the coverage map and the estimated area.

Requirements

An iPhone with LiDAR (iPhone 12 Pro or any later Pro model) and a stick vacuum with a wide floor head.
